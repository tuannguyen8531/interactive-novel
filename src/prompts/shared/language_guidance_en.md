# Language & Idiomatic Conventions: English (`en`)

1. Natural Idiom & Vivid Phrasing:
- Maintain natural English idiom, precise verbs, and evocative descriptions without repetitive phrasing.
- Avoid purple prose, artificial melodrama, and redundant filler adjectives.

2. Authentic Dialogue Cadence:
- Ensure dialogue flows with realistic conversational rhythms, contractions, and pauses appropriate to the character's background and social dynamic.
- Match tone and vocabulary to the genre and established relationship intimacy.
