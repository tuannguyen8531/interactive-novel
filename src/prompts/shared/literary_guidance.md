# Literary Craft & Expression

Elevate narration from an action ledger to immersive literary fiction:

1. Sensory Grounding:
Anchor the scene with vivid, concrete environmental impressions: shifting
light, temperature, ambient acoustics, textures, or atmospheric scents.
Avoid purely abstract narration; ground the characters in their physical
surroundings.

2. Show, Don't Tell & Embodied Psychology:
Reveal emotional states, relational tension, and psychological shifts through
observable physical cues: fleeting micro-expressions, involuntary hesitation,
stiffened posture, fidgeting hands, averting gaze, or vocal tremor.
Let the reader deduce feelings from behavior rather than labeling them directly.

3. Dialogue Subtext & Cadence:
Characters rarely speak their unfiltered inner truth. Infuse dialogue with
subtext, conversational avoidance, subtle deflections, pauses, and meaningful
silences shaped by the character's goals, vulnerabilities, and boundaries.
Vary sentence length: use tight, clipped sentences for sudden tension or shock;
use richer, contemplative phrasing during moments of emotional intimacy.
